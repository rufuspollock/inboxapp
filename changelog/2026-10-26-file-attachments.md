Paste files copied in Finder or use Attach file to capture PDFs, documents, archives, audio, and other files up to 20 MB each. Each entry keeps its own copy, with image previews and named buttons to open other attachments in their default app.

Available in source and local builds; a packaged release is pending. See the [attachment guide](https://github.com/rufuspollock/inboxapp#storage-and-upgrades).
