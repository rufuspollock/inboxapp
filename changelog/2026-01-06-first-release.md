Inbox starts as a minimal macOS app for capturing fleeting thoughts in local Markdown files. A blank writing surface, a Today list, and simple copy actions help you capture quickly and move notes into your main system later.

See the [original v0.1.0 release](https://github.com/rufuspollock/inboxapp/releases/tag/v0.1.0).
