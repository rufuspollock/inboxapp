Paste a screenshot or attach an image to a new or saved entry, and see a preview beside your Markdown. Each entry owns its images, so deleting a processed entry moves its attachments to Trash with it.

Available in [Inbox 0.2.4](https://github.com/rufuspollock/inboxapp/releases/tag/v0.2.4).
